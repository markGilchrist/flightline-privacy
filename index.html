FlightLine Controller – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

FlightLine Controller is the companion app for FlightLine Decoys controllers. It is published by Mark Gilchrist. This policy applies to the iOS and Android versions of the app.

Information we collect

None. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting, and it does not send any information to us or to anyone else over the internet.

Bluetooth

The app uses Bluetooth to find and connect to FlightLine controllers near you and to send them your settings (speed, on and off times, and on/off switches). It only connects to devices that identify themselves as FlightLine controllers. It does not use Bluetooth to work out your location.

On Android 11 and earlier, Android requires apps that scan for Bluetooth devices to hold the location permission. The app asks for it only for that reason and never reads your location.

Information stored on your device

The app remembers the settings you choose for each controller so they can be restored when it reconnects. These are stored only on your phone or tablet, are never shared, and are removed when you delete the app.

Children

The app does not collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
